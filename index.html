Destinations

Four countries, one trip

Most people who come to this part of the world combine two or three of these. They sit close together, flights between them are short, and the weather is good somewhere every month of the year.

Vietnam

Long and thin, so you travel it in a line. Sleep on a boat among the islands of Ha Long Bay, spend a slow week cooking and cycling around Hoi An, then go underground in Phong Nha, home to the largest cave passage ever found.

  • Ha Long Bay
  • Hoi An
  • Phong Nha caves
  • Hue

Best months: October to April in the north, February to August in the centre

Thailand

The easiest country in the region to travel. Cycle Bangkok's green lung at Bang Krachao, walk with rescued elephants near Chiang Mai, and take an early speedboat to the Phi Phi Islands before the crowds arrive.

  • Bangkok
  • Chiang Mai
  • Phuket
  • Khao Sok

Best months: November to April, especially on the Andaman coast

Thailand travel guide Thailand tours

Indonesia

Seventeen thousand islands, so pick a region. Learn to surf on Bali, see Komodo dragons in the wild from a wooden phinisi boat, or go to Raja Ampat, where scientists have counted more reef fish than anywhere else on Earth.

  • Bali
  • Komodo
  • Raja Ampat
  • Gili Islands

Best months: April to October for Bali and Komodo, October to April for Raja Ampat

Indonesia travel guide Indonesia tours

Cambodia

Often squeezed into two days, and almost everyone wishes they had stayed longer. Give Angkor three mornings, then head out by quad bike past stilt houses and rice paddies as the sun goes down.

  • Angkor Wat
  • Siem Reap
  • Countryside rides

Best months: November to February, though the green season has its fans

Before you book

Plan it in five steps

  1. Check entry rules for every countryVisa rules depend on your passport and change often. The JetlyGo visa checker covers each stop, and changing planes in a third country can need its own visa too.
  2. Pick months around the weatherRoute around the rain rather than through it. The table above is a start.
  3. Book flights in one city and out of anotherArriving in Hanoi and leaving from Bangkok saves days of backtracking.
  4. Lock in anything with limited placesLiveaboards, expedition caves and peak-season cruises fill months ahead. City tours can wait.
  5. Leave a spare dayWeather cancels boats in every one of these countries. Never schedule a flight the morning after an island trip.

Questions

What people ask us most

What is the best month to visit Southeast Asia?

November to February suits most of the region: Thailand, Cambodia and northern and southern Vietnam are dry and cooler. Bali and Komodo are best from April to October, and central Vietnam from February to August, so a trip across several countries is usually planned around the weather.

How long do I need for Vietnam, Thailand and Cambodia?

Three weeks is comfortable: about eleven days in Vietnam from north to centre, three days at Angkor, and a week split between Bangkok and Chiang Mai.

Is Bali or Thailand better for a first trip?

Thailand is easier to move around and has more variety in one country. Bali is better if you want one base with surf, rice terraces and diving close by. Plenty of people do both, since Bangkok has direct flights to Bali.

Do I need a visa for Southeast Asia?

It depends on your passport, and each country sets its own rules. Some passports enter visa free, others need an e-visa before travel. Check every country on your route before you book flights.
